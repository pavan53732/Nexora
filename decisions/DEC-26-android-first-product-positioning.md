# DEC-26 — Android-First Product Positioning

## Status

**Accepted documentation decision.**

## Context

`docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md` described Nexora as the “only” application designed specifically for Android. The repository establishes Nexora’s Android-first product intent, but the corpus does not provide independent market evidence establishing an exclusive “only application” claim.

## Decision

Nexora is positioned as an application designed specifically for Android as a first-class target, not a port or wrapper. The Product Vision must not present “only application” as a verified market fact.

## Preserved intent

This decision preserves the Android-first product differentiation, Android-native application boundary, first-class Android target, and non-port/non-wrapper intent. It does not change Nexora’s product scope, runtime architecture, Android lifecycle requirements, or implementation plan.

## Consequences

`docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md` uses qualified Android-first wording. Historical decision records remain historical evidence and do not override this current Product Vision wording.

No market-comparison study, exclusivity claim, competitor ranking, or external market fact is created by this decision.

## Authority and dependencies

Canonical product wording: `docs/PRODUCT_VISION.md`.

Supporting product context: `docs/PRODUCT_PRINCIPLES.md`, `PROJECT_SPECIFICATION.md`, and the project instruction that Nexora is a pure Android application.

This decision does not modify any existing `DEC-*` record.
